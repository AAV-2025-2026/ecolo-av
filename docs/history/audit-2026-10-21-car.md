## The short version

Run read-only from Ben's Mac on the car's network, 21 Oct, 10:00-10:30. Six of eight
machines surveyed; logins from the Keychain.

1. **The car's ROS network is split.** The localization Pi publishes the IMU (200 Hz)
   and GPS (10 Hz), but the Jetson never sees them: its lidar mapping waits on
   `/imu/data_raw` with 0 publishers. That is why 16 Oct saw no IMU or GPS.
2. **The Jetson already reads the Velodyne:** the stock `velodyne` driver (Humble), a
   lidar SLAM (MIT's spark-fast-lio) and foxglove_bridge, each a systemd service.
3. **Everything is on ROS domain 0, Humble.** Nothing of ours runs on the car.
4. **The Cortex's HUD server crash-loops:** 246 restarts, a Python package mismatch.
5. **Code on the car not on GitHub:** 18 repos with uncommitted changes, plus a TensorRT
   model and a stereo calibration that exist only on the Jetson.

## The three big questions

| # | Question | Answer |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Why was there **no IMU and no GPS data** on the car's ROS graph on 16 Oct? | **The network is split.** The localization Pi (.113) runs both drivers (`yahboom_imu_cpp` 200 Hz on `/imu/data_raw`, `aav_gnss` 10 Hz on `/aav/gps/fix`). The Jetson can't see them, and the localization Pi can't see the Jetson's lidar. Both see the topics of `.211`. A likely cause, not yet tested: the localization Pi is also on Carleton's WiFi (`wlan0`, 172.17.83.162), and its ROS traffic goes out on both. The GPS had no fix (status -1): indoors |
| 2 | Is `.211` the "localization" machine? | **No.** Localization is `.113` (hostname `localhost.localdomain`). `.211` is another Ubuntu 22.04 Pi, no login yet. It is likely what publishes `/carla/ego_vehicle/...`, `/battery_status`, `/e_stop` and runs `influxdb_subscriber`. The Motor-Pi? |
| 3 | Which machine reads the **Velodyne** today? | **The Jetson.** `velodyne.service` runs the stock driver (Humble), which holds UDP 2368, then `/velodyne_points`, `/scan`. `spark_fast_lio.service` maps with it (`/odometry`, `/path`, `/cloud_registered`). Our `lidar_driver` would collide on port 2368 while theirs runs |
