# NDT: finding the car on a saved lidar map, in Rust

* **Author:** Ben (the ask), written up 29 Oct 2026
* **Status:** Proposed (EGM review next)

Ben, 28 Oct: "I like the autoware, localization with NDT", "take ideas from that and rebuild
them in rust", "lets write our own version of it all". 29 Oct: the debug HUD "shows the
ndt or occupancy network". Research pick 2 (`docs/designs/2026-10-28_research-picks.md`);
the idea in pictures: `docs/learn-localization.md` §8 and `pixi run ndt-demo`.

## The idea in one line

Keep a **map** of the lot as little bell curves, one per 1 m cube. Every lidar turn, slide
and turn the scan until its points sit best on those curves: that pose is **where the car
is**. Published as a pose, scored against Gazebo's truth, shown on the debug HUD. It feeds
the EKF only after it wins (the trials' rule).

## Why our own (and not stock)

| Option | Why not, for now |
|---|---|
| Autoware `ndt_scan_matcher` | C++ on Humble, tied to Autoware's own libraries and messages |
| `ndt_omp`, `lidar_localization_ros2` | C++ on PCL; not in RoboStack for Jazzy as far as we know (checked: PCL itself is in our env) |
| **Ours, Rust** | Ben's call (28 Oct); small (a few hundred lines); tested on a laptop; the maths is in the papers (Biber 2003, Magnusson 2009) |

Checked against Autoware's design and numbers (Apache-2.0), cited in `PROVENANCE.md`.

## Simplifications that fit our car

| Choice | Why |
|---|---|
| Solve for **x, y, yaw** only (3 numbers, not 6) | a car on a flat lot: roll, pitch and height barely change. Points stay 3D (walls, posts, cars), so the map still sees shape |
| 1 m cubes for the map, then 0.5 m (coarse to fine) | as `ndt-demo` and Autoware do; far sight first, precision second |
| Newton's method with the papers' gradient and Hessian, our own | 3 unknowns: a 3×3 solve, no optimiser crate needed (argmin stays an option) |
| Start from the last NDT pose + the local EKF's motion since | the EKF knows how far the car moved; NDT corrects it |
| nalgebra for the maths | Apache-2.0, already picked (research picks) |

## The map

| Where | How |
|---|---|
| Gazebo (now) | `ndt-map --sim`: drive the test lot's tour, add each cloud at **Gazebo's truth pose** into one point set, thin it to 10 cm, save. Exact poses, so the map is exact: good for testing NDT itself |
| The car (later) | record a bag of the lot (`tools/record.sh`), build the map with **KISS-ICP** (MIT; Python, on the laptop, offline), align it to the GPS `map` frame, save |
| The file | a small binary file: a header (cube size, origin, frame) and x y z float32 rows; in `maps/ndt/` (git ignores it) |

## The parts

| Part | Where | ROS? |
|---|---|---|
| `ndt-core`: the map's cubes (mean, covariance), the score, its gradient and Hessian, Newton's steps, coarse to fine | `ros/localization/aav_ndt/crates/ndt-core` | no: `cargo test` |
| `ndt-node`: subscribes the cloud and the local EKF, publishes the pose; loads the map | `ros/localization/aav_ndt/crates/ndt-node` (rclrs, as `aav_spine_bridge`) | yes |
| `tools/ndt_map.py`: builds the Gazebo map from a run | `tools/` | through `tools/ros.sh --local` |

| Topic | Type | Dir |
|---|---|---|
| `/sensors/lidar/points` | PointCloud2 | in (best effort) |
| `/localization/odom/local` | Odometry | in: the motion since the last match |
| `/localization/ndt/pose` | PoseWithCovarianceStamped | out: covariance from the Hessian; large when the fit is poor |
| `/localization/ndt/status` | a small message or diagnostic: score, iterations, time | out: for the debug HUD and the scores |

Never TF on the first runs (the trials' rule). `ndt:=true` in `sim.launch.py`, checked like
`amcl:=true` (test lot only, lidar on). Scored by `controller-compare --ndt` beside AMCL and
the EKF (`drive_metrics.belief_errors`). Drawn by the debug HUD as a purple car.

## Checks

| Test | Pass |
|---|---|
| `ndt-core` on made-up points: a known shift and turn | found to 1 cm and 0.1° |
| Its gradient against numbers (finite differences) | agree to 1e-6 |
| A bad start (1.5 m, 10° off) | pulls in, as `ndt-demo` shows |
| Time per match, 28,800 points, on the Mac | under 50 ms (10 Hz lidar) |
| Gazebo, test lot tour | error vs truth: mean and worst, beside AMCL's and the EKF's |

## Order

1. `ndt-core` and its tests.
2. `tools/ndt_map.py --sim`: the test lot's map.
3. `ndt-node`, `ndt:=true`, the contract first.
4. `controller-compare --ndt`; the debug HUD's NDT layer.
5. Later: the car's map from a bag (KISS-ICP), and feeding `ekf_global`.
