## Overview

On 1 November we talked through a lot in one night. This page keeps it all in one place,
so nothing is lost, and so reviewers can ask: **are we doing too much, and in the right
order?**

The aim: **0.2 = sim-complete, car-ready.** Everything merged and tested in Gazebo, the
docs and the runbooks done. **0.3 = it drove**, after car day.

How solid we are today: every test passes (1513, 0 failures) and every profile runs in
Gazebo. **Nothing in this stack has moved the real car yet.**

## The direction for the algorithms

Today the stack has layers: localization, perception, planning, control. Each layer has a
default and some trials beside it. Ben, 1 Nov: "we still need the layers as we are
learning. Eventually we can collapse it down."

1. Keep the layers and their defaults. Score the trials against them.
2. Add learned parts one at a time **inside** a layer (the diffusion planner as a Nav2
   planner), with the classic stack as the safety guard (the "simplex" idea).
3. Only later, with enough of our own driving data, try a model that does more than one
   layer.
