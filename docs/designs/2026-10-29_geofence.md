# The geofence: an area the computer's drivers may not leave, without Nav2

* **Author:** Ben (the ask: F6 in the 26 Oct design review), written up 29 Oct 2026
* **Status:** Tentative: picked the recommended answers below, 29 Oct

The 26 Oct review, F6: "`goto` drives on GPS with no map. Only its distance and time
limits stop a runaway route." The 28 Oct research picks (pick 3, Simplex + RSS): the
monitor we're missing is "a speed and area envelope independent of Nav2".

## The idea in one line

A small node, `geofence` (`aav_safety`), watches where the global EKF says the car is,
and closes a **twist_mux lock** before the car's **stopping distance** reaches the fence's
edge. Every program stops; people still drive, and drive it back in.

## Where it sits

```
/localization/odom/global ──► geofence ──► /safety/geofence/lock ──► twist_mux lock "geofence" (51)
  (map frame: x, y, yaw,          │                                     holds scripted 50, replay 40,
   speed, covariance)             ├─► /safety/geofence/state  (OK / NEAR / OUTSIDE / LOST ... why)
                                  └─► /safety/geofence/fence  (the fence, for RViz and Lichtblick)
```

No Nav2 in the path: the point driver, the scripted driver, a replay and the language
server's drives are held the same way as Nav2.

## Choices

### 1. Where the fence lives

| Option | For | Against |
|---|---|---|
| **A circle round the start** (`radius`, centred on the first steady pose) | works today, with the placeholder datum; nothing to survey | the centre moves if the node restarts mid-drive |
| A polygon per site, map frame, in `config/geofence.yaml` (`sites.<name>`) | the lot's real shape | needs a real datum first (W3): with today's placeholder the map frame isn't on the ground |
| Both: polygon if `site` is set, else the circle | the car gets the circle now, the lot's polygon later | two code paths (small: both give "how far inside") |

**Picked: both.** The car's default is a **30 m circle**. The test lot's polygon is a
team TODO once the datum is surveyed (W3). Gazebo: a 200 m circle (the sim checks drive
up to 60 m). Bench: 100 m.

### 2. What it acts on

| Option | For | Against |
|---|---|---|
| **A twist_mux lock** | the pattern we have (e-stop, stop sign, watchdog, stop hold); people are never held | only stops: no slow-down |
| ego clamps the speed | could slow, not only stop | ego holds people too; ego would need the map pose |
| the spine | firmware is the trusted part | the spine has no position; position in firmware is a big new job |

**Picked: the lock, at priority 51.** It holds every program: scripted (50), replay (40),
point (25), Nav2 (20), AI (10). Never a person (joy 100, RC 95, keyboard 90) and never
the actuator tests (60: a person runs them, at their own 1 m/s cap, usually on stands).

### 3. When the fence can't be trusted

| Situation | State | Lock |
|---|---|---|
| no global pose yet, or still settling (`settle_time` 3 s) | `STARTING` | **on** |
| the last pose is older than `max_age` (1 s) | `LOST` | **on** |
| position spread (1 σ) over `max_std` (3 m), or NaN | `LOST` | **on** |
| the node itself dies or hangs | twist_mux's lock timeout (1 s) | **on** |

**Picked: lost means stop**, as the sensor watchdog does. `LOST` lets go by itself once
good poses come back: then the fence is trusted again.

### 4. Speed near the edge: the stopping distance (RSS)

The lock closes when any corner of the car, **moved on by its stopping distance**, comes
within `margin` + σ of the edge:

```
d = v·ρ + v² / (2·b)          ρ = reaction_time 0.6 s   (lock tick 0.1 + ego's cmd_timeout 0.3 + the spine)
                              b = braking 1.0 m/s²     (half of ego's 2.0: what we count on)
at 1 m/s: d = 0.6 + 0.5 = 1.1 m, plus margin 1.0 m, plus σ
```

So a faster car is stopped earlier: that's the speed limit near the edge. No separate
slow zone (ego already caps 1 m/s); a slow zone is a later question (G4).

### 5. How a person drives back in

| State | Means | Lets go |
|---|---|---|
| `NEAR` | the stopping point is within margin of the edge | by itself, once every corner is `hysteresis` (0.5 m) further in |
| `OUTSIDE` | base_link itself is outside the fence | **latched**: a person drives back in, then `pixi run geofence-clear` (refused while still outside) |

People are never held, so the safety driver takes the joystick, turns the car and drives
it in. A program can't creep along the edge: at a standstill the corners alone (d = 0)
must be margin + hysteresis inside.

## Questions (picked the recommended answer)

| # | Question | Picked |
|---|---|---|
| G1 | Circle, polygon, or both? | both: the circle now (30 m on the car), the lot's polygon after W3 |
| G2 | Lock, ego or spine? | a twist_mux lock at 51: every program, never a person or the actuator tests |
| G3 | Localization lost? | stop (lock), until good poses come back |
| G4 | A slow zone near the edge? | not now: the stopping distance moves the edge in with speed; ego caps 1 m/s |
| G5 | Latch? | only `OUTSIDE` latches (a person clears it); `NEAR` and `LOST` let go by themselves |
| G6 | Fail-safe on silence? | yes: lock timeout 1 s, like the watchdog's; `mux.launch.py geofence_lock:=false` where no geofence runs (the turtle, CARLA) |
| G7 | Can a launch turn it off on the car? | no: `car.launch.py` starts it wherever it starts the mux |

## What changes on the car by default

- Every program stops 30 m (minus its stopping distance and margin) from where the car
  stood still at start-up. People don't.
- Every program stops while the global EKF is silent, over 3 m unsure, or not yet settled.
- twist_mux holds every program if the geofence node isn't running.

## Build steps

1. `aav_safety/geofence.py` (no ROS): `Circle`, `Polygon`, `stopping_distance`,
   `Geofence` (the states above). Its tests.
2. The node `geofence.py` → `geofence_node`; `config/geofence.yaml`; `launch/geofence.launch.py`.
3. twist_mux's `geofence` lock; `mux.launch.py geofence_lock`; the contract.
4. Launch wiring: car (with the mux), bench, sim (`geofence:=true`, its radius).
5. `bench-check --geofence`: a scripted drive at 1 m/s toward a 12 m fence must stop
   inside it; then the keyboard (a person) backs it in, and the lock opens.
6. Docs: `docs/geofence.md`, the package README; TODO item 5; features.md.
