Everything flows one way: sensors, then "where am I", then drivers, the mux, ego, and one backend. The layers only talk through ROS topics, so any box can be swapped without touching the others.

The picture

1. Sensors: raw data

Sensor Driver Publishes
Wheel speed today encoder_udp (in aav_legacy); later the spine /vehicle/odom
IMU (Yahboom, USB) yahboom_imu_cpp, then the stock imu_filter_madgwick /imu/data_raw, then /imu/data
GPS (u-blox, USB) the stock ublox_dgnss /gps/fix
The car's shape the stock robot_state_publisher, from vehicle.yaml /tf_static: where each sensor sits
Lidar, cameras next year

Each driver publishes a standard message in its own frame. Another IMU or GPS can replace it without changing anything downstream.

2. Where am I: two filters

  • ekf_local fuses the wheels and the IMU. It's smooth and never jumps, but it drifts slowly, like counting your steps. Use it to steer.
  • ekf_global fuses the same inputs plus GPS. It stays near the truth, but can hop a little when a GPS fix arrives. Use it to plan.
  • navsat_transform turns GPS latitude and longitude into map x and y for ekf_global.

Does the global EKF read the local one?

In effect, yes. ekf_global publishes only the correction map → odom, worked out on top of ekf_local's odom → base_link. Chained together, they give the car's true position, and the local estimate stays smooth.

3. Drivers propose, the mux picks

Every driver publishes a TwistStamped (speed in m/s, turn rate in rad/s) on its own topic. None of them knows what kind of vehicle it's driving.

Driver Topic Priority
Joystick: the safety driver /cmd_vel/joy 100
Keyboard, or another remote /cmd_vel/remote 90
Scripted: speed up, hold, slow down /cmd_vel/scripted 50
Replay of a recorded drive /cmd_vel/replay 40
Nav2: drive to a point on the map /cmd_vel/nav 20
AI /cmd_vel/ai 10, later

twist_mux passes on the highest priority that has spoken in the last 0.5 s, as /cmd_vel. Publishing true on /estop blocks every driver until someone publishes false.

Teleop is just another driver. The keyboard's commands take the same path as Nav2's: mux, ego, then the turtle or the car.

4. ego: the virtual car

ego is the only node that knows the car is a car. Fifty times a second it turns /cmd_vel into /ego/actuator_cmd: an AckermannDriveStamped with a speed and a steering angle (positive is left).

Step What it does
Bicycle model steering angle = atan(wheelbase × turn rate ÷ speed). A car can't turn on the spot, so at zero speed ego holds the wheels where they are.
Limits 1.0 m/s top speed to start; acceleration; steering ±0.5 rad; steering rate
Timeout no command for 0.3 s: brake to a stop

5. One car model, everywhere

The car's numbers live in one file, src/aav_description/config/vehicle.yaml. The launch files hand them to every part that needs them.

Part Uses
ego the wheelbase and steering limit, for the bicycle model
URDF (robot_state_publisher) where each sensor sits
Nav2 the footprint, and the tightest turn: wheelbase ÷ tan(max steer) = 2.86 m
turtlesim_adapter, gazebo_adapter the wheelbase, so the turtle and the simulated car turn like the car
aav_spine, aav_legacy the wheelbase, track and wheel size

Most of the numbers are still guesses: see Car numbers.

6. Backends: one plays the car

Backend Package Does
The real car aav_spine /ego/actuator_cmd into CAN frames, for the spine firmware
Today's car aav_legacy into UART frames, for the 2025-26 ESP32 firmware
turtlesim aav_turtlesim the stock turtlesim, steered with the bicycle model
Gazebo aav_gazebo a simulated car with an IMU and GPS, on the real car's topics
CARLA aav_carla a stub, for next year

Each backend reports the car's speed back on /vehicle/odom. That closes the loop into the EKFs.

7. Safety layers

From the person outward. Each layer works even if everything after it fails.

# Layer Stops the car when
1 Joystick deadman (priority 100) held with the sticks centred
2 /estop it's true: the mux blocks every driver, and the car backends brake
3 ego's timeout no command arrives for 0.3 s
4 spine_bridge ego is quiet for 0.1 s: it stops sending drive frames
5 The spine's watchdog no valid frame for 200 ms: throttle off, brake on
6 RC override switch, hardware e-stop, driver faults always: handled in the firmware

Today's car has no layer 5

The 2025-26 ESP32 firmware has no timeout. If the bridge dies, the last throttle stays on. Keep the wheels off the ground and the e-stop in hand: see Firmware.
