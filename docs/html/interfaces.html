/drivers/nav/route/path nav_msgs/msg/Path street_router map - Transient local (latched). The whole route in the map frame (each point through /fromLL). The map window (map, aav_hud_raylib) draws it violet under Nav2's blue plan; RViz and Foxglove can too. Not a plan Nav2 follows.
/drivers/nav/route/status std_msgs/msg/String street_router - - Transient local (latched). For people - the route's length and the router's time for a normal car, or why there's no route (no router, no GPS fix, longer than max_length).

/diagnostics diagnostic_msgs/msg/DiagnosticArray twist_mux, ekf_local, ekf_global, sensor_watchdog language_drive - ROS's diagnostics. sensor_watchdog reports each input it watches once a second (OK, WARN, ERROR when it stops the car); for the wheels, also the gaps to the IMU (speed_gap, turn_gap, worst_speed_gap, worst_turn_gap), to tune the cross check on the car. twist_mux reports each lock once a second ("lock locks.estop" locked or free) - the only place a node started after an e-stop can learn it, since /safety/estop is sent a few times and its senders go away. language_drive refuses to move while it says locked, or while it is silent. pixi run check ignores the topic.
