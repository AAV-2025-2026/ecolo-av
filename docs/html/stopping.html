On this page:1. The stopping law2. The behaviour planner3. Where the car really stops4. A heavier car: the adaptive brake

One stopping law for every planned stop, a behaviour planner that uses it to slow Nav2, and a check of where the car really stops. Ben, 7 Nov: "we can use that to say if we have turned enough or slowing down enough". The designs: how the car stops, behaviour and speed.

Every number is a GUESS until car tests 4.1-4.5 (actuator tests). The locks don't use any of this: they keep their own laws.

1. The stopping law

   S(v)  =  v·τ  +  v²/(2a)           τ = ρ + a/(2j)
            └┬─┘    └──┬──┘
       delay + easing  braking

   the cap:     v_cap(d) = a·( √(τ² + 2d/a) − τ )     the speed that stops in d metres

aav_description stop_law.py (no ROS): StopCurve(a, j, rho) with distance(v), cap(d), demand(v), h(room, v, margin).

Its numbers come from the car model

Symbol Means From Today
a planned braking half the softer of ego's max_deceleration and max_brake 1.0 m/s²
j jerk ego's max_jerk 2.0 m/s³
ρ every delay input age 0.15 s + brake dead time + brake_lag + the speed loop's lag 1.22 s
τ delay + easing ρ + a/(2j) 1.47 s

The speed loop's lag (new, not in the design): the spine's default PI coasts until the car is 0.2 m/s too fast, then brakes 0.5 per m/s past that. To brake at 1 m/s² it must be 0.87 m/s too fast: 0.87 s behind. Without it, the law overran the point by 0.3-0.4 m on the pretend car. loop=None gives the design's ρ 0.35.

2. The behaviour planner

aav_behaviour (a trial, off by default): pixi run sim world:=road behaviour:=true. It knows why the car should slow, and sends Nav2 a speed cap. It can't stop the car: a lock or Nav2's goal check makes every final stop.

3. Where the car really stops

pixi run sim-check --stop-point (the empty world, the spine in the loop, about 4 minutes).

Step What happens
1 a scripted drive at 0.5, 0.75 and 1 m/s (the spine's top speed: 1 m/s)
2 steady for 1.5 s, then the e-stop: the spine brakes fully
3 the prediction: the first /ego/stop_point after the press (ego, the shared car model, full brake)
4 moved to the map by /tf at its stamp
5 scored against Gazebo's truth and the global EKF

Pass: within 0.25 m, or 10 % of the distance if more. A drive that never reached half its speed fails. The maths is tools/stop_points.py: it can score a bag from the car later, with a tape measure for the truth.

4. A heavier car: the adaptive brake

Ben, 8 Nov: "if we brake without a person vs with a person ... we are going to need to dynamically realize that we aren't braking as fast as we expect and start applying more force. We don't want to overwhelm the brakes though."

The same brake stops a heavier car more slowly (a = F / m). With 150 kg aboard, the 181 kg car brakes at 55 % of its empty strength.

Where it sits

 ego ─▶ speed loop ─▶ brake asked ─▶ adaptive brake ─▶ brake sent ─▶ brake actuator
                                         ▲
                                   wheel speed

 e-stop, stale command, silent spine ─▶ full brake   (the locks: never through it)

Below ego and every safety lock: it only adds to what the speed loop asks.

What it does, each tick

Step What
1 measures the slowing from the wheel speed
2 works out the slowing the empty car model expects for the brake sent
3 learns the effectiveness: measured ÷ expected (1 empty, 0.55 with 150 kg)
4 brakes harder by 1 ÷ effectiveness (at most 3 times)
5 the extra rises at most 2 (full brake) a second
6 never above the safe max
7 near the safe max for longer than max hold time: the extra comes off

It keeps what it learnt from stop to stop: a person aboard stays aboard.

The brake's limits (vehicle.yaml, brake:)

Like the steering's ends. Placeholders until car day A7, rows 6-8 (car day).

Key Placeholder Means
released 400 the brake actuator's target with the brake let go
touch 800 where the pads start to grip
full 3500 full brake
safe_max 3200 the most the brake may ever be pushed
max_hold_time 5 s how long near safe_max

released and full must match the firmware's (calibration.rs); a test checks.

A stop from 2 m/s at 1 m/s², four times. The prediction is always the empty car model's. Pass: within 0.25 m.
