Where things stand early on 11 October 2026. "Works" means it ran under ROS 2 Jazzy through Pixi: on oirp-spss (Linux), and since 10 October on Ben's Mac too. "On the bench" means Bench mode: the car's own software, with stand-ins for the hardware. "Untested" means the code and its config exist, but it hasn't run yet. The plan and the work left are in Work.

Is it all on Jazzy?

Yes. Everything builds against Jazzy, and the turtle, sim and replay profiles run. Every configuration key was checked against what the running Jazzy nodes accept. The fixes were small: clean shutdowns, Nav2 on simulated time, and the simulated GPS noise. The details are in docs/history/audit-2026-10-10-*.md.
