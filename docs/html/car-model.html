How the car answers a command: one model, in Python and Rust, for ego, the simulators and the drivers. Ben, 7 Nov: "we really want the ego to have a mathematical model for steering, braking and acceleration ... have the algorithms know about lag".

Every number is a GUESS until car tests 3.10-3.12 and 4.1-4.5 (actuator tests). The dead times are 0 until measured.

Where it lives

What Where

Rust twin ros/bringup/aav_description/car-model/ (crate car-model)

Old import path aav_gazebo.actuator_model still works: the same objects

Why aav_description: it already holds vehicle.yaml and vehicle.py, and ego and Gazebo both depend on it. The model is part of "what the car is".

The picture

 command ──▶ dead time ──▶ lag ──▶ speed ──────────────┐
 (pedals or                                            ├──▶ bicycle ──▶ x, y, yaw
  a set speed)                                         │
 steering ─▶ dead time ─▶ deadband ─▶ motor rate ─▶ linkage lag ─▶ + offset ─┘

A set speed goes through the spine's speed loop first (Python: the port; Rust: spine-core's own).

One step of dt

# Part Maths
1 Dead time each input waits floor(dead_time / dt + 0.5) steps
2 Throttle, brake level' = (u - level) / lag, stepped exactly: level += (u - level)(1 - e^(-dt/lag))
3 Speed v += max_accel · throttle · dt (0 below the deadband), then brake and drag take (max_brake · brake + drag · abs(v)) · dt off, never past 0
4 Steering setpoint moves only past steer_deadband; motor at most steer_rate · dt; linkage lags steer_lag; plus steer_offset; within ±max_steering
5 Bicycle yaw += v tan(steer) / wheelbase · dt; x += v cos(yaw_mid) dt, y += v sin(yaw_mid) dt

v and steer in step 5 are the step's new ones; yaw_mid is halfway between the old and new yaw.

CarState CarState

predict gives a state every dt for horizon s, the first one dt ahead. The last command is held. The speed loop is copied, never advanced.

ego's lead

The actuators answer late. ego can ask a little ahead, so the car answers about when the driver wanted (aav_ego lag_lead.py).

driver's request ──▶ filter (lag_lead_filter) ──▶ + T × its rate ──▶ ego's limits ──▶ car
Lead T
speeding up throttle_dead_time + throttle_lag
slowing brake_dead_time + brake_lag
steering steer_dead_time + steer_lag

The car then answers as if its lag were lag_lead_filter (0.1 s), not T. A dead time can't be undone (it would need the future): it counts as more lag. The steering motor's rate limit isn't a lag: nothing undoes it.

When it runs

lag_lead Simulator (use_sim_time) Real car
auto (default) on off
on on on: only after tests 3.10-3.12 and 4.1-4.5
off off off

Never while a lag-aware driver drives, whatever the setting. Change it live: ros2 param set /ego lag_lead off.

Still a safety lock

Rule How
Every limit still applies the lead runs before max_speed, the steering limit and rate, the slews, the jerk, the body
Small at most lag_lead_max_speed (0.3 m/s) and lag_lead_max_steer (0.1 rad)
No change of direction forward stays forward, reverse stays reverse
A slow-down is never softened while slowing, never above the request
A stop is a stop no fresh command: no lead

On the car model with today's guesses, the lead follows a speed ramp about 6% closer: the spine's speed loop is most of the lag, and the lead can't change that loop.

Lag-aware drivers: the contract

A driver that leads for the lag itself (an MPC or MPPI that uses this model) tells ego, so the lag isn't led for twice.

Step The driver does
1 publish true on /drivers/<slot>/lag_aware (std_msgs/Bool, latched: reliable, transient local, depth 1) when its lag-aware mode starts; false when it ends
2 stamp every TwistStamped with now
3 that's all: ego does the rest

How ego knows who is driving: twist_mux only publishes /diagnostics, not the winner. But it passes the winner's message on unchanged. So ego keeps each lag-aware driver's last 50 commands and looks for the one on /control/cmd_vel (same stamp and twist).

Slot Who will publish it
nav aav_track's lag-aware trackers, the Rust MPPI (both drive through Nav2)
scripted, point, ai nobody yet

The slots ego watches are its lag_aware_drivers parameter.

ego's trace

Where the car is going, like a tail but in front (Ben, 7 Nov). /ego/trace, nav_msgs/Path, 10 Hz (aav_ego trace_ahead.py).
Horizon 2 s, a pose every 0.1 s (21 poses)
Frame base_link, at the header's stamp
Starts from ego's twin of the car: the model, stepped with what ego sent, its speed the last /vehicle/odom
Runs a copy of ego (lead, ramps, limits) with the request held, into the car model

Why base_link: ego needs no localization (stands, the turtle). A driver turns the trace into odom with TF at the stamp, then matches it against its plan.
