Carleton in Gazebo, on one Nav2 costmap, with a guessed NDT map. It's for practice while the hardware catches up (Ben, 5 Nov). Nothing here has met the real campus yet.

pixi run campus                                       # builds maps/campus/ (about 10 s)
pixi run sim world:=campus reversing:=false screen_cubes:=true ndt:=true gui:=false rviz:=false
pixi run dev-window --live --ndt-map maps/ndt/campus.pcd   # in a second terminal

Click a road or lot on the map, then confirm, as in any world. The car starts in lot P4, because the datum itself is on a station shelter. It drives at up to 2.5 m/s here, in Gazebo only: the real car's limit stays 1 m/s.

What's real and what's guessed

From OpenStreetMap Guessed by tools/campus_world.py
where roads, lots, buildings and trees are how tall most buildings are (529 of 574)
their shapes where kerbs run (58 km: every edge of every road and lot, 0.30 m)
many road widths other road widths, tree sizes, trees inside woods
all the looks: pavement, grass, brick, stone

The data is (c) OpenStreetMap contributors, under the ODbL, so anything showing it must say so. maps/ isn't in git: pixi run campus makes it again.

Google: being students doesn't change Google's terms, which limit copying their maps into another map. So the world is built from OpenStreetMap. Your own tape measurements and points you read by hand are for checking it.

What it makes

File What it is
maps/campus/campus.sdf, meshes/, textures/ the world: 355,000 triangles, bump-mapped
maps/campus/campus.pgm, .yaml one Nav2 map: 1.6 x 1.6 km at 0.25 m (6400 x 6400 cells)
maps/campus/place.yaml where the car starts, what went in, the credit
maps/ndt/campus.pcd NDT's map, a guess: points on the same shapes

The shape (Ben, 5 Nov): everything that isn't pavement is a foot up, held in by a concrete kerb, with grass on top.

One costmap, no segments. It works on a Mac: only roads and lots are free. Gazebo held real time (0.98-1.01). Nav2's planner loop runs at about 8 Hz, not the 20 Hz it wants, and warns about it in the console. Paths and grass are blocked: the car stays on roads and lots.

Sent once. At this size Nav2 sending the whole costmap every second froze the window (up to 1.66 s) and may have caused the flashing. world:=campus sends it once, then only changes. The window also skips a grid it already has.

What was measured (Gazebo, 5 Nov)

This doesn't show the real campus will work. The guess is made from the same shapes as the world, so of course it fits here.

NDT's score floor

ndt.yaml's 2.3 is the test lot's, with walls close by. On the campus, fits at the true place score about 1.6-1.9 on the guess, and 2.00-2.31 on a recorded map. So this world uses 1.5.

Floors. The grass is at 0.30 m, right on the car's usual 0.3 m floor. NDT looked at it and got lost, so here NDT looks above 0.45 m. The window's cubes look down to 0.12 m, so kerbs and grass show as the lidar's rings. Nav2's flat scan keeps 0.3 m: untested against kerbs (TODO 119).

Not measured: how high a wrong pose scores here. Until it is, leave ndt_ekf off on the campus: NDT is watched, and doesn't steer the estimate.
